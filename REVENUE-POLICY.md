# Commons Revenue Policy

## 1. Purpose

Money received through organizational licensing exists to sustain the Commons, not to purchase control over it.

## 2. Organizational License Revenue

Organizational license fees enter the Commons treasury and may support:

* Commons infrastructure;
* administration;
* governance;
* community programs;
* accessibility;
* documentation;
* grants;
* development;
* stewardship; and
* other purposes authorized through Commons governance.

## 3. Creator Participation Revenue

Money owed because particular registered works are used in a Compensated Bundle is treated separately as Creator Participation money.

Creator Participation money should not be absorbed into general Commons revenue except for a published administrative deduction approved through Commons governance.

## 4. Transparency

The Commons should publish periodic aggregate reporting including:

* organizational licensing revenue;
* amounts waived;
* Creator Participation Pools created;
* amounts distributed to creators;
* administrative costs; and
* Commons expenditures.

Private contractual, personal, or commercially sensitive information need not be publicly disclosed.

## 5. Governance

Financial contribution does not purchase additional governance power.
