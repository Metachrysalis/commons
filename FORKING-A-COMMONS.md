# Creating Your Own Commons

The Metachrysalis Commons framework is intended to be reusable.

A community creating a Commons should decide:

## Identity

* Commons name
* purpose
* steward
* governance structure

## Participation

* who may join
* admission process
* responsibilities
* departure and removal process

## Rights

* who may Share
* who may Adapt
* Commons-Alike requirements
* Contribute-Back requirements

## Organizational Use

* whether organizational authorization is required
* who may approve organizations
* financial thresholds
* waiver rules
* licensing term

## Creator Economics

* whether direct creator compensation applies
* Creator Pool percentage
* permitted commercial weights
* administration rules

## Governance

A fork should replace Metachrysalis-specific names and links.

Use of this framework does not imply endorsement by or affiliation with Metachrysalis.

Communities are encouraged to preserve a visible record of how their Commons rules evolve over time.
