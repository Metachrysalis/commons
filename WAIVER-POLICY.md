# Organizational License Waiver Policy

## Purpose

Financial requirements should sustain the Commons without excluding valuable participation merely because money is unavailable.

## Available Arrangements

The Commons may approve:

* a full waiver;
* a partial waiver;
* deferred payment;
* staged payment; or
* an agreed contribution-in-lieu.

## Relevant Considerations

The Commons may consider:

* actual ability to pay;
* public or community benefit;
* humanitarian or emergency-response purpose;
* work benefiting resource-constrained communities;
* education and research;
* geographic and economic disparities;
* experimental or pilot use;
* substantial contribution back to the Commons;
* accessibility work;
* translation;
* documentation;
* stewardship;
* infrastructure support; and
* other circumstances relevant to the purposes of the Commons.

Nonprofit status does not automatically produce a waiver.

For-profit status does not automatically prevent one.

## Contribution-in-Lieu

Possible contributions include:

* translations;
* accessibility improvements;
* documentation;
* scenario development;
* software development;
* facilitation;
* training;
* research;
* community support;
* hosting or infrastructure; and
* other contributions agreed to in writing.

Contribution-in-lieu arrangements must be agreed before being treated as satisfaction of a monetary obligation.

## Effect

Unless expressly stated otherwise, a waiver changes only the financial requirement.

It does not waive Commons-Alike, Contribute-Back, attribution, membership, Creator Participation, or other obligations.
