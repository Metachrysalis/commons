# Metachrysalis Sustaining Organization License 0.1

## 1. Purpose

The Metachrysalis Commons permits broad individual participation while asking organizations with meaningful access to resources to contribute toward sustaining the Commons when they derive organizational value from Commons Material.

A Sustaining Organization License supplements the Players Commons and Creators Commons licenses.

It does not replace them.

## 2. Organizational Use

**Organizational Use** means exercising Commons rights substantially for, through, on behalf of, at the direction of, or for the benefit of an organization, business, institution, public body, association, funded project, or other organized entity.

Organizational Use may include:

* internal organizational use;
* training;
* facilitation;
* consulting;
* funded research;
* program delivery;
* product or service development;
* client-facing activities;
* conferences or workshops;
* organizational planning; and
* other activities undertaken in an organizational capacity.

An individual's Commons membership does not by itself authorize Organizational Use.

Uses that do not require copyright permission under applicable law remain unaffected.

## 3. Application Required

An organization wishing to undertake covered Organizational Use must apply for a Sustaining Organization License.

Publication of an application process, licensing terms, fee schedule, or eligibility criteria is an invitation to apply and does not constitute an offer that can be accepted merely by application or payment.

No Sustaining Organization License exists until affirmatively issued by the authorized Commons body.

## 4. Discretionary Approval

The Commons may:

* approve an application;
* approve it with limitations;
* approve only some requested uses;
* approve a project or organizational unit rather than an entire organization;
* establish conditions;
* offer a fee waiver or reduction;
* propose contribution-in-lieu;
* defer the application; or
* decline the application.

Subject to applicable law, the Commons may decline an application without providing reasons.

The Commons may consider any relevant circumstances, including the applicant's purposes, conduct, intended use, relationships with affected communities, ability to comply with Commons responsibilities, and alignment with the continued integrity and flourishing of the Commons.

## 5. Fees

Approval and pricing are separate determinations.

Qualification for a particular fee tier does not create entitlement to a license.

Organizations falling within a zero-fee tier must still apply and receive approval.

Fees are determined according to `ORGANIZATIONAL-TERMS.md` unless varied in the issued license.

## 6. Individual Membership

A Sustaining Organization License does not confer Commons membership.

Individuals exercising Players rights for the organization must independently hold applicable Players Commons rights.

Individuals exercising Creators rights for the organization must independently hold applicable Creators Commons rights.

## 7. Creator Compensation

Authorization for Organizational Use does not extinguish Creator Participation obligations.

When an organization conducts a Compensated Bundle using registered Creators Commons works, the organization must comply with the applicable Creator Participation Pool rules.

Organizational licensing fees and Creator Participation payments are distinct unless the issued license expressly combines them.

## 8. Commons-Alike and Contribute-Back

Payment does not permit enclosure.

Adaptations remain subject to Commons-Alike and Contribute-Back obligations.

## 9. No Exclusivity

A Sustaining Organization License is non-exclusive unless the Commons and all affected rights holders expressly agree otherwise.

No organizational license grants ownership of underlying Commons Material.

## 10. Term

Each license states:

* licensed organization;
* authorized organizational unit or project;
* permitted uses;
* effective date;
* expiry date;
* applicable financial terms; and
* special conditions.

## 11. Renewal

Renewal is not automatic.

The Commons may decline renewal without providing reasons except where otherwise required by law.

Past licensing does not create an entitlement to future licensing.

## 12. Suspension and Termination

A license may be suspended or terminated during its stated term for:

* material breach;
* unauthorized use;
* failure to make required payment;
* material misrepresentation in the application;
* failure to meet an explicit condition of the license; or
* another termination ground expressly contained in the issued license.

Expiration or termination does not ordinarily make completed lawful acts retroactively unlawful.

## 13. No Endorsement

Licensing does not imply that Metachrysalis or any Commons member endorses the organization or its activities.
