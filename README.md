# Metachrysalis Commons

The Metachrysalis Commons is a family of membership-based commons for sharing, adapting, and stewarding creative work.

The framework distinguishes between:

1. **Players Commons** — members may Share Commons Material.
2. **Creators Commons** — members receive Players rights and may also Adapt Commons Material.
3. **Sustaining Organizations** — organizations may apply for authorization to exercise Commons rights in an organizational context.

Commons participation by individuals is not purchased.

Organizations with meaningful financial resources are normally expected to contribute financially when they receive organizational value from Commons Material.

Organizational authorization is discretionary. Ability or willingness to pay does not create a right to receive a license.

## Core Principle

**People participate in the Commons. Organizations sustain it.**

Money may support participation in the Commons, but it does not purchase control over the Commons.
