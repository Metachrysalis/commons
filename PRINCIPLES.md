# Metachrysalis Commons Principles

## 1. The Commons Grows Through Use

Commons Material should be usable, shareable, and capable of becoming the foundation for further creation.

## 2. Reciprocity

Those who benefit from the Commons should contribute to its continued flourishing according to their capacity.

Contribution may take the form of creative work, facilitation, stewardship, money, knowledge, infrastructure, care, translation, documentation, or other forms recognized by the Commons.

## 3. Individual Participation Is Not Pay-to-Play

Membership rights are not purchased.

Financial contribution does not confer additional creative rights, governance votes, priority, ownership, or status.

## 4. Organizational Capacity Carries Organizational Responsibility

Organizations deriving organizational value from the Commons may be required to obtain a Sustaining Organization License and make an appropriate financial or equivalent contribution.

## 5. Organizational Licensing Is Discretionary

No organization is entitled to receive a Sustaining Organization License.

The Commons retains the freedom, subject to applicable law, to decide which organizations it will enter into licensing relationships with.

Licensing revenue never creates an obligation to enter into or continue a relationship that the Commons does not wish to maintain.

## 6. Creators Retain Recognition and Agency

Creators retain ownership of their contributions except where they explicitly agree otherwise.

Contributors may determine the commercial compensation weight assigned to their registered works within the framework established by the Commons.

## 7. Commons-Alike

Adaptations created through Commons rights remain available to the Commons under equivalent Commons rights.

## 8. Contribute-Back

Substantive adaptations made through Commons rights should return to the Commons rather than becoming inaccessible improvements held only by their creator.

## 9. Compensation Follows Contribution

When specific Commons contributions are incorporated into compensated bundles, creators who have elected to participate in creator compensation should share appropriately in the resulting Creator Participation Pool.

## 10. Money Buys Permission, Not Privilege

Payment by an organization does not:

* confer Commons membership;
* provide governance authority;
* permit enclosure of Commons Material;
* extinguish attribution or compensation obligations;
* create exclusivity; or
* entitle the organization to future licensing.
