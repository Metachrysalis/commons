# Sustaining Organization Terms

**Draft economic schedule — subject to revision before 1.0**

## 1. Pricing Principle

Fees should reflect an organization's capacity rather than simply its legal form.

Nonprofit, charitable, governmental, cooperative, academic, and commercial status do not by themselves determine ability to pay.

## 2. Suggested Annual Fee Bands

| Annual Organizational Resources | Annual License | Project License |
| ------------------------------- | -------------: | --------------: |
| Under CAD $250,000              |             $0 |              $0 |
| $250,000–$1 million             |           $500 |            $250 |
| $1–5 million                    |         $1,000 |            $500 |
| $5–25 million                   |         $2,500 |          $1,250 |
| $25–100 million                 |         $5,000 |          $2,500 |
| Over $100 million               |       $10,000+ |         $5,000+ |

All amounts are starting points.

An organization's placement in a fee band does not guarantee approval.

## 3. Organizational Resources

Depending on context, Organizational Resources may refer to:

* consolidated gross business revenue;
* annual nonprofit operating revenue;
* annual operating expenditure;
* the budget of a genuinely independent department;
* the budget of a funded project; or
* another reasonable indicator of financial capacity.

The Commons may prevent artificial subdivision of an organization undertaken principally to reduce licensing fees.

## 4. Scope

A license may cover:

* one activity;
* one project;
* one program;
* one organizational unit; or
* an entire organization.

Pricing should reflect the scope actually authorized.

## 5. Self-Certification

Applicants ordinarily self-certify the applicable financial band.

The Commons may request reasonable supporting information where necessary.

Confidential financial records need not be publicly posted.

## 6. No Payment Before Approval

Applicants should not send licensing fees before approval.

Payment does not cause approval.
